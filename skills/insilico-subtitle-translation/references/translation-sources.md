# Translation sources

Read the current project sources in this order:

1. Project-specific approved subtitles, scripts, glossaries, legal or regulatory material, and current official company material for the relevant recording.
2. `英矽智能中英专名与固定译法.md` for company, platform, project, and proper-name spelling.
3. `英矽智能中英翻译与校对手册.md` for scientific, clinical, business, and evidence-strength language.
4. General industry usage only when the first three sources do not resolve the term.

Do not treat time-dependent facts, such as clinical stage, regulatory status, company statistics, partner relationships, or pipeline status, as static terminology. Preserve the recording's claim unless a correction is verified by material from the relevant period.

Key invariants:

- `Insilico Medicine` is `英矽智能`; retain platform brand capitalization and punctuation.
- Keep AI-discovered targets distinct from AI-designed molecules and drug development.
- Preserve qualifiers including `may`, `potential`, `trend`, `signal`, `exploratory`, and conditional commercial language.
- Never translate an IND clearance as market approval.
