Use this reference for all Insilico Medicine translation and proofreading. Official project-specific or legally required wording overrides this list.

Preserve registered legal entity names and `ddb.insilico.com` exactly unless a source itself requires a translated display name. Do not alter domain spelling, punctuation, capitalization, or the internal formatting of official names.

For English SRT proofreading, preserve `dddbench.insilico.com` exactly as written. It is an approved English website/domain reference only: do not translate it into Chinese, add a Chinese name, or introduce it unless it appears in the source audio or approved English reference.

## Projects, generic names, and targets

- Preserve IDs exactly: `ISM001-055`, `INS018_055`, `ISM3091`, `XL309`, `ISM5043`, `ISM8207`.
- Do not normalize a hyphen to an underscore or treat historical and partner IDs as universally interchangeable.
- Preserve target symbols: `TNIK`, `USP1`, `KAT6A`, `QPCTL`, `MAT2A`, `ENPP1`, `DGKA`, `CDK12`, `TEAD`, `FGFR2/3`, `KIF18A`, `WRN`, `PHD2`, `3CLPro`.
- Keep `rentosertib` in English until an authoritative Chinese generic name is verified. In English prose, generic drug names are normally lowercase.
- Recommended patterns: `TNIK抑制剂`, `靶向TNIK的候选药物`, `USP1抑制剂ISM3091`, `rentosertib（原项目编号ISM001-055）`.

## Evidence language

Preserve qualifiers:

- `may/might` → `可能／或可`
- `potential/potentially` → `潜在／有望`
- `suggest/indicate` → `提示／表明`
- `emerging trend` → `初步显现的趋势`
- `encouraging results` → `令人鼓舞的结果`
- `well tolerated` → `耐受性良好`, not `安全有效`
- `no new safety signals` → `未发现新的安全性信号`, not `无安全风险`

Statistical significance, clinical meaningfulness, biomarker change, and clinical benefit are separate claims. Preserve study-design and limitation language.
